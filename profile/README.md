## About Us

The Alloy Theory Lab studies how physics, atomic structure, defects, interfaces, and lattice dynamics govern the behavior of metals, alloys, and disordered materials.

We integrate theory, computational modeling, and experiments to understand fundamental materials behavior and design materials for nuclear, aerospace, energy, and other extreme environments.

## Research Areas

- Phase stability, chemical short-range order, and alloy design
- Interstitial–solute interactions, diffusion, and crystal defects
- Grain boundaries, segregation, complexions, and amorphization
- Lattice dynamics, phonons, and thermal transport
- Refractory alloys, oxidation, and radiation effects

## Research Approaches

- First-principles calculations (DFT)
- Molecular dynamics and atomistic modeling
- Machine learning and machine-learning interatomic potentials
- Thermodynamic modeling and CALPHAD
- Alloy synthesis, processing, and materials characterization
