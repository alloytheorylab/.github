## About us

The Alloy Theory Lab studies how chemistry, atomic structure, defects, interfaces, and lattice dynamics govern the behavior of metals, alloys, and disordered materials. We integrate theory, computational modeling, and experiments to understand fundamental materials behavior and design materials for nuclear, aerospace, energy, and other extreme environments.

## Research areas

- Phase stability, chemical short-range order, and alloy design
- Interstitial–solute interactions, diffusion, and crystal defects
- Grain boundaries, segregation, complexions, and amorphization
- Lattice dynamics, phonons, and thermal transport
- Refractory alloys, oxidation, and radiation effects

## Research approaches

First-principles calculations (DFT), molecular dynamics, machine learning, interatomic potentials, CALPHAD, alloy synthesis, and materials characterization.

## Open research resources

- [MagMaster](https://alloytheorylab.github.io/magmaster.html) — an interactive dataset of binary magnesium compounds based on DFT calculations and machine learning.
- [Lab website repository](https://github.com/alloytheorylab/alloytheorylab.github.io) — source code for our website and associated public resources.

Research codes, datasets, interatomic potentials, and computational workflows will be shared here as they become available.
